Facilities

Everything you need for a comfortable stay — and a hall for the day you want to remember.

Restaurant & bar

The hotel’s restaurant serves breakfast buffet, à la carte or continental, and lunch and dinner through the day. There is a bar on site, a snack bar and a coffee house, and breakfast can be served in your room. Sit on the covered terrace under the veranda, or in the garden.

Grilled fish with chips is one of the dishes guests photograph most — see it in the gallery.

Weddings & conferences

The hotel’s conference and banquet halls are a popular choice for weddings, funerals, conferences and church programmes across the Upper West Region. The hotel can arrange the hall, the catering and the rooms for your guests in one booking.

Illustrative image The hall photo shown here is a temporary stand-in — it is not a photo of the hotel’s event space. A real photo will replace it.

To plan an event, call 055 130 5021 or send a message on WhatsApp.

Garden & terrace

A paved walkway runs through the hedges and tall trees to the hotel, and the covered terrace — wicker chairs, red cushions, the arched veranda — is the quietest place in Wa to sit with a drink in the evening. The shared lounge and TV area are indoors if you prefer shade.
