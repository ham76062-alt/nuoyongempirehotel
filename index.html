The hotel of the Upper West

Nuoyong Empire Hotel has welcomed guests to Wa since April 2015. Set in the calm Sombo Residential Area, the hotel is a garden retreat with a restaurant, a bar and halls that host some of the region’s biggest weddings and conferences.

The hotel was named Hotel of the Year at the Upper West Regional Tourism Awards — an honour the team is proud to hold. Whether you are in Wa for business, a family celebration or a night on the road, you will find a clean, comfortable room, a warm meal and people who look after you.
