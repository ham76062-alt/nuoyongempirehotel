Getting here

From Wa Airport: about 16 minutes’ drive. The hotel offers a free airport shuttle — ask when you book, or call 055 130 5021 when you land.

By road: the hotel is on Mase Road in the Sombo Residential Area, off Wa-Mase Road near Times Baptist Church. There is free private parking on site.

From the town centre: a short drive from the Wa market and the main lorry station.
