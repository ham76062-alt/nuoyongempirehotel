Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Nuoyong Empire Hotel (Mase Road 15, Sombo Residential Area, Wa, Upper West Region, Ghana) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you send an enquiry through this website, you give us your name, phone number and any details you type into the form — for example your dates of stay and special requests. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

How we use it

We use the information you give us only to answer your enquiry, confirm rates and availability, and arrange your stay or event. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

WhatsApp enquiries

Enquiries sent from this website open in WhatsApp and are delivered to the hotel’s WhatsApp number. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to messages sent through WhatsApp.

Website analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to correct or delete the information you have given us. Contact us on 055 130 5021 or by email at nuoyongempirehotel2015@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
